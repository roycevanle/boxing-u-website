Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign up?

Sign up via PushPress—first, 1st session is free! At arrival, you'll sign waiver, we'll help you wrap your hands, and let you know what to expect. Arrive 15-20 minutes early if it is your 1st time.

What do I need to bring to a training session?

Bring your own hand wraps and gloves. We'll have gloves/wraps to borrow & for purchase.

Are the classes suitable for beginners?

All of our sessions, with the exception of sparring, are open to beginners and first-time boxers. Boxing University Gym is a welcoming place for boxers of all skill and fitness levels.

When are payments due?

Monthly payments are due the 5th of each month. Privates and individual classes are due at time of session.

Do I need to get hit?

No.

When are y'all closed?

Saturdays. Moreover, we will be closed on certain holidays & certain days where we have special events or emergancies. Be sure to check out our Instagram for updates. Contact us at 206-825-5714 if you have any questions.
